              How priorities are set

              Screening thresholds per municipality and scenario (assumptions, adjustable in pipeline/build_risks.py). People = residents of flood cells.

              • No dry route: 🔴 ≥ 1,000 people, or ≥ 30% and ≥ 200 · 🟠 ≥ 200, or ≥ 10% and ≥ 50 · 🟡 any
              • No usable shelter while people live in flood cells: 🔴
              • Shelter capacity gap (people ÷ rough usable capacity): 🔴 > 100% · 🟠 > 50% · 🟡 > 25%
              • Overloaded shelters (people above capacity): 🔴 ≥ 1,000 or ≥ 50% · 🟠 ≥ 200 or ≥ 20% · 🟡 any
              • Elderly (65+) in flood cells: 🔴 ≥ 1,000 · 🟠 ≥ 300 · 🟡 any
              • No step-free route (PRM): 🔴 ≥ 500 · 🟠 ≥ 100 · 🟡 any
              • Walk > 30 min to a dry shelter: 🔴 ≥ 500 · 🟠 ≥ 100 · 🟡 any
              • Homes with no dry floor (height ≤ cell depth + 1 m): 🔴 ≥ 500 · 🟠 ≥ 100 · 🟡 any
              • Hospital in the extent: 🔴 not usable · 🟠 upper floors only
              • School in the extent: 🟠 not usable · 🟡 upper floors only
              • Main roads / railway lines flooded: 🟠 ≥ 20% of the mapped network · 🟡 any
              • Dams or weirs within ~2 km, data gaps (no modelled river, < 50% mapped): 🟡

              The thresholds are screening assumptions from the team's analysis, not official standards. Planning support, not official evacuation guidance.
